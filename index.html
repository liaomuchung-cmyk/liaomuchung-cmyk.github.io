牧群小秘書（Muqun Secretary）

本應用程式為廖牧群個人使用的行事曆與郵件助理，僅供本人使用，不對外提供服務。

This application is a personal calendar and email assistant used solely by its owner, Mu-Chun Liao. It is not offered to the public.

  • Google Calendar：讀取與建立本人行程 / read and create the owner's own events
  • Gmail：僅唯讀，用於整理本人每日信件摘要 / read-only, for the owner's daily email digest
